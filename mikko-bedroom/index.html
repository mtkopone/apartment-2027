Headboard Wall

Interactive 3D model of the floor-to-ceiling headboard wall from the designer's elevation, with the ideas for the space behind the headboard as switchable features. Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom, switch between day and evening light, open the cabinet doors and dim the mirror LED.
